# Meddy Design Skill

Use this skill whenever designing or styling any Meddy screen or component.

## Visual Identity

Meddy uses a clean white and soft pink design system.

### Colors

Background:

- #FFFFFF

Soft pink surface:

- #FFF6F9

Primary pink:

- #F28BA8

Strong pink:

- #E96F93

Light pink:

- #FCE1E9

Dark text:

- #2B2B2B

Secondary text:

- #737373

Border:

- #F1DCE3

Success:

- #73B98C

Warning:

- #E8AA55

Danger:

- #DF7070

Pink is an accent color.
Do not make the whole screen strongly pink.

## UI Style

Use:

- clean white backgrounds
- large rounded cards
- subtle shadows
- soft pink highlights
- generous spacing
- high readability
- simple icons
- large touch targets

Avoid:

- dense dashboards
- excessive gradients
- glassmorphism
- tiny buttons
- tiny text
- hospital-like styling
- overly dark screens

## Accessibility

Meddy is primarily designed for elderly users.

Important UI must have:

- large readable text
- high contrast
- clear labels
- simple wording
- large touch targets
- obvious primary actions

Do not rely only on color for statuses.

Prefer:

- ✓ Taken
- ⏰ Upcoming
- ! Missed

## Home Screen

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Greeting
2. Meddy hero area
3. Next medicine
4. Primary Taken action
5. Today's progress
6. Today's schedule
7. Quick actions
8. Care Circle preview

The user should immediately understand:

- what medicine is next
- when to take it
- what to press after taking it

## Cards

Cards should generally use:

- white or soft pink surfaces
- rounded corners
- comfortable padding
- simple layouts
- subtle borders or shadows

Avoid excessive nested cards.

## Buttons

Primary actions:

- soft or strong pink
- high contrast
- large
- rounded

Secondary actions:

- white/light pink
- bordered
- visually less dominant

## Navigation

Primary tabs:

- Home
- Medicines
- Care Circle
- Profile

Keep navigation simple and clearly labeled.
